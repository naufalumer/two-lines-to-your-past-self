Two lines to my past self, 256 characters: one concrete moment, Tuesday 4 June 2019 at 9:40 a.m., when Mrs. Hartono asks me to stay after class and read my essay aloud, and the reason it mattered, those ten minutes are why I am still writing seven years later. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty, $2 in USDC.

> On Tuesday 4 June 2019 at 9:40 a.m., when Mrs. Hartono asks you to stay after class and read your essay aloud, stay.
> Those ten minutes are the first time you hear your own words sound like a writer, and they are why you are still writing seven years later.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the two lines byte-exact (no trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Standard library only, no network, no randomness, no clock: every run produces identical output.
